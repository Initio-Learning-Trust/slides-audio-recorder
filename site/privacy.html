Privacy policy

Last updated 8 September 2026.

The short version

Audio Recorder for Slides records audio in your browser and saves it to your own Google Drive. We do not operate a server that receives your recordings, and we cannot see them, your slides, or anything else in your Google account.

Who we are

Audio Recorder for Slides is published by Initio Learning Trust. For anything in this policy, contact us at [insert contact address].

What the add-on accesses

PermissionWhy
The presentation you have open (presentations.currentonly) To add a play control to the slide you are on, and to read the slide list so recordings can be labelled with the right slide number. No other presentation is accessible.
Files the add-on creates (drive.file) To save your recordings, apply the sharing you choose, rename them and move them to the bin when you ask. The add-on cannot see any other file in your Drive.
Show a sidebar (script.container.ui) The add-on's entire interface.
Make outbound requests (script.external_request) Used only to call the Google Drive API. The add-on's manifest restricts outbound requests to https://www.googleapis.com/, so it cannot contact anywhere else.

What happens to a recording

  1. Audio is captured by the recording window running in your browser, using the microphone you allow. Nothing is uploaded while you record.
  2. When you press Use recording, the audio is passed from that window to the add-on sidebar inside Google Slides, in your browser.
  3. The sidebar sends it to the add-on's Google Apps Script code, which writes it into your own Google Drive.

Recordings are stored in your Google Drive, under your Google account, subject to Google's terms and your organisation's policies. They are not copied anywhere else.

The recording window

The recording window is a static page served from our website. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts and no tracking of any kind. It sets no cookies, writes no browser storage, holds no sign-in credentials, and makes no network requests once it has loaded. Standard web-server request logs may record that the page was fetched, in the same way as for any web page.

Sharing

The add-on applies the sharing you choose in Settings to each recording as it is saved: your organisation, anyone with the link, or private. Changing this setting affects new recordings; existing files keep the sharing they were given until you change it. You can change any recording's sharing yourself in Google Drive at any time.

Please consider your school's own policies before sharing recordings of pupils' voices, and before recording anyone without their knowledge.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, process or transmit your recordings, your presentations, your name, your email address or any usage analytics. There is no account to create and no sign-in to our systems, because there are no systems of ours to sign into.

The add-on stores small amounts of information inside your own Google account: your preferences (recording quality, sharing choice, button style) and a list of the recordings made in each presentation. These are held in Google's Apps Script properties for your account and document, and are removed when you uninstall the add-on or delete the presentation.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Audio Recorder for Slides's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not transfer this data to anyone, use it for advertising, or allow humans to read it.

Removing the add-on

Uninstall it from Extensions ▸ Add-ons ▸ Manage add-ons, or revoke its access at your Google account permissions page. Your recordings stay in your Drive; delete them there if you want them gone.
